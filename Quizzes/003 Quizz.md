## Quiz 3 - Preguntas y Respuestas

---

**1. MC.08.56**  
Two methods of accounting for uncollectible accounts are the  
- [ ] a. direct write-off method and the allowance method  
- [ ] b. allowance method and the net realizable method  
- [ ] c. allowance method and the accrual method  
- [ ] d. direct write-off method and the accrual method

**Correct answer: a. direct write-off method and the allowance method**

---

**2. MC.09.65**  
Accumulated Depreciation  
- [ ] a. is a contra asset account  
- [ ] b. is the same as Depreciation Expense  
- [ ] c. is used to show the amount of cost expiration of intangibles  
- [ ] d. is used to show the amount of cost expiration of natural resources

**Correct answer: a. is a contra asset account**

---

**3. MC.08.54**  
Under the direct write-off method of accounting for uncollectible accounts, Bad Debt Expense is debited  
- [ ] a. when a credit sale is past due  
- [ ] b. at the end of each accounting period  
- [ ] c. when an account is determined to be worthless  
- [ ] d. whenever a predetermined amount of credit sales have been made

**Correct answer: c. when an account is determined to be worthless**

---

**4. MC.08.57**  
The operating expense recorded from uncollectible receivables can be called all of the following except  
- [ ] a. uncollectible accounts expense  
- [ ] b. doubtful accounts expense  
- [ ] c. bad debt expense  
- [ ] d. bad receivables expense

**Correct answer: d. bad receivables expense**

---

**5. MC.08.74**  
An aging of a company's accounts receivable indicates an estimate of uncollectible accounts of $7,900. If Allowance for Doubtful Accounts has a $700 credit balance, the adjustment to journalize the bad debt expense for the period will require a  
- [ ] a. debit to Bad Debt Expense for $7,900  
- [ ] b. credit to Allowance for Doubtful Accounts for $700  
- [ ] c. debit to Bad Debt Expense for $8,600  
- [ ] d. debit to Bad Debt Expense for $7,200

**Correct answer: d. debit to Bad Debt Expense for $7,200**

---

**6. MC.10.41**  
Notes may be issued  
- [ ] a. to creditors to temporarily satisfy an account payable created earlier  
- [ ] b. when assets are purchased  
- [ ] c. when borrowing money  
- [ ] d. all of these choices

**Correct answer: d. all of these choices**

**7. MC.10.42**  
Which of the following will be reported as a long-term liability on the balance sheet?  
- [ ] a. accounts payable  
- [ ] b. accrued liabilities  
- [ ] c. notes payable (due in 11 months)  
- [ ] d. none of these choices

**Correct answer: d. none of these choices**

---

**8. MC.12.91**  
The date on which a cash dividend becomes a binding legal obligation is on the  
- [ ] a. date of record  
- [ ] b. payment date  
- [ ] c. declaration date  
- [ ] d. last day of the fiscal year

**Correct answer: c. declaration date**

---

**9. MC.12.80**  
Par value  
- [ ] a. represents what a share of stock is worth  
- [ ] b. is established for a share of stock after it is issued  
- [ ] c. represents the original selling price for a share of stock  
- [ ] d. is the monetary value assigned to a share of stock in the corporate charter

**Correct answer: d. is the monetary value assigned to a share of stock in the corporate charter**

---

**10. MC.08.67**  
What is the type of account and normal balance of Allowance for Doubtful Accounts?  
- [ ] a. contra asset, credit  
- [ ] b. asset, credit  
- [ ] c. asset, debit  
- [ ] d. contra asset, debit

**Correct answer: a. contra asset, credit**

---

**11. MC.08.53**  
The direct write-off method of accounting for uncollectible accounts  
- [ ] a. emphasizes the matching of expenses with revenues  
- [ ] b. emphasizes cash realizable value  
- [ ] c. is often used by small companies and companies with few receivables  
- [ ] d. emphasizes balance sheet relationships

**Correct answer: c. is often used by small companies and companies with few receivables**

**12. MC.09.60**  
A characteristic of a fixed asset is that it is  
- [ ] a. held for sale in the ordinary course of the business  
- [ ] b. used in the operations of a business  
- [ ] c. a short-term investment  
- [ ] d. intangible

**Correct answer: b. used in the operations of a business**

**13. MC.10.59**  
A current liability is a debt that is reasonably expected to be paid  
- [ ] a. between 6 months and 18 months  
- [ ] b. out of cash currently on hand  
- [ ] c. out of currently recognized revenues  
- [ ] d. within 1 year

**Correct answer: d. within 1 year**

**17. MC.12.83**  
If common stock is issued for an amount greater than par value, the excess should be credited to  
- [ ] a. Cash  
- [ ] b. Legal Capital
