# **Question:** 1

**Question (English):**
Which of the following accounts is a stockholders' equity account?

a. Accounts Payable
b. Prepaid Insurance
c. Cash
d. Common Stock

**Answer (English):**
**d. Common Stock**

**Explanation:**
Common Stock is a **stockholders' equity account** because it represents the owners' investment in the company.

* Accounts Payable → Liability
* Prepaid Insurance → Asset
* Cash → Asset
* Common Stock → Stockholders' Equity ✅

---

**Pregunta (Español):**
¿Cuál de las siguientes cuentas es una cuenta de capital contable (Stockholders' Equity)?

a. Accounts Payable (Cuentas por Pagar)
b. Prepaid Insurance (Seguro Pagado por Adelantado)
c. Cash (Efectivo)
d. Common Stock (Acciones Comunes)

**Respuesta (Español):**
**d. Common Stock (Acciones Comunes)**

**Explicación:**
Common Stock es una cuenta de **capital contable** porque representa la inversión de los propietarios en la empresa.

* Accounts Payable → Pasivo
* Prepaid Insurance → Activo
* Cash → Activo
* Common Stock → Capital Contable ✅

# **Question:** 2

**Question (English):**
Which side of the account increases the cash account?

a. neither a debit nor a credit
b. debit
c. credit
d. either a debit or a credit

**Answer (English):**
**b. debit**

**Explanation:**
Cash is an **asset account**. Asset accounts increase with a **debit** and decrease with a **credit**. Therefore, a debit increases the cash account.

---

**Pregunta (Español):**
¿Qué lado de la cuenta aumenta la cuenta de efectivo (Cash)?

a. ni un débito ni un crédito
b. débito
c. crédito
d. ya sea un débito o un crédito

**Respuesta (Español):**
**b. débito**

**Explicación:**
Cash (Efectivo) es una cuenta de **activo**. Las cuentas de activo aumentan con un **débito** y disminuyen con un **crédito**. Por lo tanto, un débito aumenta la cuenta de efectivo. ✅

# **Question:** 4

**Question (English):**
Which of the following types of accounts have a normal credit balance?

a. revenues and common stock
b. assets and liabilities
c. liabilities and expenses
d. common stock and dividends

**Answer (English):**
✅ **a. revenues and common stock**

**Explanation:**
Accounts with a normal **credit balance** include:

* **Liabilities**
* **Common Stock (Stockholders' Equity)**
* **Revenues**

Accounts with a normal **debit balance** include:

* **Assets**
* **Expenses**
* **Dividends**

Therefore, both **revenues** and **common stock** normally have credit balances.

---

# **Question:** 8

**Question (English):**
The assets and liabilities of a company are $128,000 and $84,000, respectively. Stockholders' equity should equal:

a. $84,000
b. $212,000
c. $128,000
d. $44,000

**Answer (English):**
✅ **d. $44,000**

**Explanation (English):**
Using the accounting equation:


$$
\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Stockholders' Equity}
$$

$$
128,000 = 84,000 + \text{Stockholders' Equity}
$$

$$
\text{Stockholders' Equity} = 128,000 - 84,000 = 44,000
$$



---

a. $84,000

d. $44,000

**Respuesta (Español):**
✅ **d. $44,000**

**Explicación (Español):**
Usando la ecuación contable:

$$
\text{Activos} = \text{Pasivos} + \text{Capital Contable}
$$

$$
128,000 = 84,000 + \text{Capital Contable}
$$

$$
\text{Capital Contable} = 128,000 - 84,000 = 44,000
$$



✅ **Respuesta correcta: d. $44,000**

# **Question:** 9

**Question (English):**
Which of the following entries journalizes the receipt of cash for 2 months' rent? The cash was received in advance of providing the service.

a. debit Cash; credit Prepaid Rent
b. debit Prepaid Rent; credit Rent Revenue
c. debit Cash; credit Rent Expense
d. debit Cash; credit Unearned Rent

**Answer (English):**
✅ **d. debit Cash; credit Unearned Rent**

**Explanation (English):**
When cash is received in advance for rent (before earning it), it is recorded as a liability called **Unearned Rent**.

* Cash increases → Debit Cash
* Liability increases → Credit Unearned Rent

---

a. débito a Cash; crédito a Prepaid Rent
b. débito a Prepaid Rent; crédito a Rent Revenue
c. débito a Cash; crédito a Rent Expense
d. débito a Cash; crédito a Unearned Rent

**Respuesta (Español):**
✅ **d. débito a Cash; crédito a Unearned Rent**

**Explicación (Español):**
Cuando se recibe efectivo por adelantado (antes de prestar el servicio), se registra como un **pasivo (liability)** llamado **Unearned Rent (renta no ganada)**.

* Cash aumenta → Débito
* Pasivo aumenta → Crédito

✅ **Respuesta correcta: d. debit Cash; credit Unearned Rent**

# **Question:** 10

**Question (English):**
Cash dividends:

a. increase expenses
b. decrease expenses
c. increase cash
d. decrease stockholders' equity

**Answer (English):**
✅ **d. decrease stockholders' equity**

**Explanation (English):**
Cash dividends are distributions to owners. They are **not expenses**. Instead, they reduce **Retained Earnings**, which decreases **Stockholders' Equity**.

---

**Pregunta (Español):**
Los dividendos en efectivo:

a. aumentan los gastos
b. disminuyen los gastos
c. aumentan el efectivo
d. disminuyen el capital contable (stockholders' equity)

**Respuesta (Español):**
✅ **d. disminuyen el capital contable**

**Explicación (Español):**
Los dividendos en efectivo no son gastos. Son distribuciones a los dueños y reducen las **utilidades retenidas (retained earnings)**, lo cual disminuye el **capital contable**.

✅ **Respuesta correcta: d. decrease stockholders' equity**
