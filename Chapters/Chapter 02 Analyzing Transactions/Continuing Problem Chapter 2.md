## Calculation of Retained Earnings [Cálculo de las Ganancias Retenidas]

**Step 1: Accounting Equation [Paso 1: Ecuación Contable]**

$$
\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Stockholders' Equity}
$$

$$
\text{Activos} = \text{Pasivos} + \text{Patrimonio de los Accionistas}
$$

**Step 2: Calculate Total Assets [Paso 2: Calcular los Activos Totales]**

| Asset Account | Amount |
|---------------|--------|
| Cash | $3,920 |
| Accounts Receivable | $1,000 |
| Supplies | $170 |
| **Total Assets** | **$5,090** |

**Step 3: Calculate Total Liabilities [Paso 3: Calcular los Pasivos Totales]**

| Liability Account | Amount |
|-------------------|--------|
| Accounts Payable | $250 |
| **Total Liabilities** | **$250** |

**Step 4: Calculate Total Stockholders' Equity [Paso 4: Calcular el Patrimonio Total de los Accionistas]**

$$
\text{Stockholders' Equity} = \text{Assets} - \text{Liabilities}
$$

$$
\text{Patrimonio de los Accionistas} = \text{Activos} - \text{Pasivos}
$$

$$
\text{Stockholders' Equity} = \$5,090 - \$250 = \$4,840
$$

**Step 5: Calculate Retained Earnings [Paso 5: Calcular las Ganancias Retenidas]**

$$
\text{Stockholders' Equity} = \text{Common Stock} + \text{Retained Earnings}
$$

$$
\text{Patrimonio de los Accionistas} = \text{Acciones Comunes} + \text{Ganancias Retenidas}
$$

$$
\text{Retained Earnings} = \text{Stockholders' Equity} - \text{Common Stock}
$$

$$
\text{Ganancias Retenidas} = \text{Patrimonio de los Accionistas} - \text{Acciones Comunes}
$$

$$
\text{Retained Earnings} = \$4,840 - \$4,000 = \$840
$$

---

## Verification of Accounting Equation [Verificación de la Ecuación Contable]

$$
\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Common Stock} + \text{Retained Earnings}
$$

$$
\text{Activos} = \text{Pasivos} + \text{Acciones Comunes} + \text{Ganancias Retenidas}
$$

$$
\$5,090 = \$250 + \$4,000 + \$840
$$

$$
\$5,090 = \$5,090 \quad \text{✓ BALANCES / CUADRA}
$$
