# Gross profit

During the current year, merchandise is sold for $186,500 cash and $1,437,200 on account. The cost of the goods sold is $900,000.  
What is the amount of the gross profit?

[Durante el año actual, se vende mercancía por $186,500 al contado y $1,437,200 a crédito. El costo de los bienes vendidos es de $900,000.  
¿Cuál es el monto de la utilidad bruta?]

---

## Solution [Solución]

| | Amount |
|--|--|
| Cash sales [Ventas al contado] | $186,500 |
| Sales on account [Ventas a crédito] | 1,437,200 |
| **Total sales** [**Ventas totales**] | **$1,623,700** |
| Cost of goods sold [Costo de bienes vendidos] | (900,000) |
| **Gross profit** [**Utilidad bruta**] | **$723,700** |

---

**Calculation:**

[**Cálculo:**]

Total Sales = Cash Sales + Sales on Account
Total Sales = $186,500 + $1,437,200 = **$1,623,700**

Gross Profit = Total Sales – Cost of Goods Sold
Gross Profit = $1,623,700 – $900,000 = **$723,700**

---

**Answer:** **$723,700**
